Running into a problem, or have a question about Eight Bars? The fastest way to reach me is by opening an issue on GitHub.

Open a support request

Before you file

Search existing issues first — your question may already be answered.

What helps me help you

  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • Steps to reproduce it, if it's a bug — which song, which screen, what you tapped
  • Your device, its OS version, and the app version (Settings, in the app)
  • A screenshot, if it's visual

Common questions

  • Splitting a song is taking a while. It runs on your device, so it depends on the device: about fifteen seconds for a four-minute song on a recent iPhone. Leave the app in the foreground while it works — iOS will not let it use the GPU in the background, and the app picks up where it left off next time you open the song.
  • An Apple Music song will not slow down or split. Streamed songs are played through Apple's own player, which can change the speed but nothing else. Import a file you own to loop, split or transpose it.
  • Nothing to play with yet? Tap "Try a demo song" on the library screen.
