Privacy Policy

Eight Bars · Last updated 27 September 2026

Eight Bars collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Nobody — including the developer — receives your songs, your practice, or any record of how you use the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your songs. Audio you import is copied into the app's own storage on that device.
  • The stems. Splitting a song into drums, bass, other and vocals runs on your device with Apple's Core ML. Audio is never uploaded for processing.
  • Chords, key, tempo, tablature and lyrics. All worked out on the device. Lyrics use Apple's on-device speech recognition on the separated vocal track.
  • Your loops, marks, sections and settings.
  • Microphone input. If you turn on playing along, your instrument or voice is mixed into your headphones live. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your song list, loops, marks and settings sync between your own devices through Apple's CloudKit, in the private database of your own iCloud account. The developer cannot see it. Audio files are not synced; they stay on the device that holds them. You can turn sync off in the app's settings.

Apple's services

Some features ask Apple for something, and are subject to Apple's own privacy policy:

  • Apple Music. With your permission, the app reads the music in your library and can play an Apple Music song at practice speed. Playback goes through Apple's own player.
  • Identify a song. The app sends a short acoustic signature of the playing audio to Apple's Shazam service to name the song. The signature cannot be turned back into audio, and no recording is kept.
  • Speech recognition. Used on the device for lyrics.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page will say so, with the date above updated.
