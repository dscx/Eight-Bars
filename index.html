Eight Bars

Loop, slow down, split stems.

Eight Bars is for the four bars you cannot play yet. Bookmark the passage, slow it down without changing the pitch, and loop it until it is yours.

Split a song into drums, bass, other and vocals on your own device — nothing is uploaded — then mute the part you play and play it yourself. The key, the tempo, the chords and a bar grid you can nudge are all worked out on the device too, along with tablature and lyrics from the vocal stem.

For iPhone, iPad and Mac. No account, no ads, nothing collected. Privacy policy · Support
